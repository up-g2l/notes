Ch. 1–2 Counting & Probability — Formulas

Equally likely outcomes: \[P(A)=\frac{|A|}{|S|}\]
Permutations / Combinations / Multinomial: \[P(n,r)=\frac{n!}{(n-r)!} \qquad \binom{n}{r}=\frac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!} \qquad \frac{n!}{n_1!\cdots n_k!}\]
Inclusion-Exclusion: \[P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)\]
Complement & Difference: \[P(A^c)=1-P(A) \qquad P(A\setminus B)=P(A)-P(A\cap B)\]

Q1 — Counting Problems [Ch. 1–2 · 3 pts each]

(a) What fraction of all shortest grid paths from \(A(0,0)\) to \(B(7,5)\) pass through \(C(3,2)\)?
(b) Six faculty are chosen uniformly at random, one per assignment. What is the probability all six are from different departments (out of 11)?
(c) A 3-member bench is chosen at random from 9 justices (6 conservative, 3 liberal). Find the probability all three share the same ideology.
(d) All 9 justices stand in a line for a photo. Find the probability no two liberal justices are adjacent.
(f) A bag has 6 red and 5 blue balls. Three are drawn without replacement. Find \(P(\text{at least one red})\).
(g) A fair coin is tossed 5 times. Find \(P(\text{more heads than tails})\).

Q1 — Solutions (a)–(d)

(a) A→C: \(\binom{5}{2}=10\); C→B: \(\binom{7}{3}=35\); total A→B: \(\binom{12}{5}=792\)
\(P=\dfrac{10\times35}{792}=\dfrac{175}{396}\approx0.4419\)
(b) Sample space: \(11^6\); favorable: \(P(11,6)=11\cdot10\cdot9\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6\)
\(P=P(11,6)/11^6\approx0.1878\)
(c) All conservative: \(\binom{6}{3}=20\); all liberal: \(\binom{3}{3}=1\); total: \(\binom{9}{3}=84\)
\(P=21/84=1/4\)
(d) Arrange 6 conservatives (\(6!\) ways) → 7 gaps; place 3 liberals in distinct gaps: \(P(7,3)=210\)
\(P=\dfrac{6!\times P(7,3)}{9!}=\dfrac{720\times210}{362{,}880}=5/12\)

Q1 — Solutions (f)–(g)

(f) \[P(\text{no red})=\frac{\binom{5}{3}}{\binom{11}{3}}=\frac{10}{165}\]
\(P(\ge1\text{ red})=1-10/165=31/33\approx0.9394\)
(g) More H than T \(\Leftrightarrow X\ge3\) where \(X\sim\text{Bin}(5,\tfrac12)\): \[P(X\ge3)=\frac{\binom53+\binom54+\binom55}{2^5}=\frac{10+5+1}{32}=\frac{16}{32}\]
\(P=1/2\)

Ch. 3 Conditional Probability & Bayes — Formulas

Conditional probability: \[P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}\]
Multiplication rule: \[P(A\cap B)=P(A\mid B)\,P(B)=P(B\mid A)\,P(A)\]
Law of Total Probability (partition \(A_1,\ldots,A_n\)): \[P(B)=\sum_{i}P(B\mid A_i)\,P(A_i)\]
Bayes' Formula: \[P(A_j\mid B)=\frac{P(B\mid A_j)\,P(A_j)}{\displaystyle\sum_{i}P(B\mid A_i)\,P(A_i)}\]
Independence: \[A\perp B \;\Leftrightarrow\; P(A\cap B)=P(A)\,P(B) \;\Leftrightarrow\; P(A\mid B)=P(A)\]

Q1 — Conditional Probability Problems [Ch. 3 · 3 pts each]

(e) A fair die is rolled 5 times. Given that the first and last rolls sum to 5, find \(P(\text{all five rolls show different numbers})\).
(h) \(P(E)=0.6\), \(P(F)=0.5\), \(P(F\mid E)=0.4\). Find \(P(E\cup F)\).
(i) \(A\perp B\), \(P(A)=0.5\), \(P(B)=0.6\). Find \(P(\text{exactly one of }A,B\text{ occurs})\).
(j) Urn I: 5 red + 5 white. Urn II: 12 white. One ball is transferred from Urn I to Urn II at random; then one ball is drawn from Urn II. Find \(P(\text{white drawn})\).

Q1 — Solutions (e), (h)–(j)

(e) Pairs \((d_1,d_5)\) summing to 5: 4 pairs; \(|B|=4\times6^3=864\). All different: \(4\times4\times3\times2=96\).
\(P=96/864=1/9\)
(h) \(P(E\cap F)=0.4\times0.6=0.24\)
\(P(E\cup F)=0.6+0.5-0.24=0.86\)
(i) \(P(\text{only }A)+P(\text{only }B)=0.5\times0.4+0.5\times0.6\)
\(P=0.20+0.30=0.50\)
(j) Urn II has 13 balls after transfer. By LTP: \(P(W_2)=\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac{12}{13}+\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac{13}{13}=\tfrac{12}{26}+\tfrac{13}{26}\)
\(P=25/26\approx0.9615\)

Q3 — Medical Testing: PPV & NPV [10 pts · Ch. 3]

A medical test has:
  • Prevalence: \(P(D^+)=0.15\)
  • False positive rate: \(P(T^+\mid D^-)=0.20\)
  • False negative rate: \(P(T^-\mid D^+)=0.10\)
Find the Positive Predictive Value \(=P(D^+\mid T^+)\) and the Negative Predictive Value \(=P(D^-\mid T^-)\).

Q3 — Solutions

Sensitivity \(P(T^+\mid D^+)=0.90\); Specificity \(P(T^-\mid D^-)=0.80\).
Law of Total Probability: \[P(T^+)=0.90\times0.15+0.20\times0.85=0.135+0.170=0.305\]
PPV: \[\text{PPV}=\frac{0.90\times0.15}{0.305}=\frac{0.135}{0.305}\]
\(\approx0.4426\)
NPV: \(P(T^-)=1-0.305=0.695\) \[\text{NPV}=\frac{0.80\times0.85}{0.695}=\frac{0.680}{0.695}\]
\(\approx0.9784\)

Ch. 4 Random Variables, Expectation & Variance — Formulas

PMF & CDF: \[p(x)=P(X=x),\quad\sum_x p(x)=1,\quad F(x)=P(X\le x)=\sum_{k\le x}p(k)\]
Expectation: \[E[X]=\sum_x x\,p(x),\qquad E[g(X)]=\sum_x g(x)\,p(x)\]
Variance: \[\text{Var}(X)=E[X^2]-(E[X])^2, \qquad \text{SD}(X)=\sqrt{\text{Var}(X)}\]
Linear transformations & sums: \[E[aX+b]=aE[X]+b, \quad \text{Var}(aX+b)=a^2\text{Var}(X)\] \[E[X+Y]=E[X]+E[Y], \quad \text{Var}(X+Y)=\text{Var}(X)+\text{Var}(Y)\;(X\perp Y)\]

Ch. 4–5 Discrete Distributions — Formulas

Binomial\((n,p)\): \[P(X=k)=\binom nk p^k(1-p)^{n-k},\quad\mu=np,\quad\sigma^2=np(1-p)\]
Poisson\((\lambda)\): \[P(X=k)=e^{-\lambda}\frac{\lambda^k}{k!},\quad\mu=\sigma^2=\lambda\]
Geometric\((p)\) (# trials to 1st success): \[P(X=k)=(1-p)^{k-1}p,\quad\mu=1/p,\quad\sigma^2=(1-p)/p^2\]
Negative Binomial\((r,p)\) (# trials for \(r\)-th success): \[P(X=k)=\binom{k-1}{r-1}p^r(1-p)^{k-r},\quad\mu=r/p\]
Hypergeometric (sample \(n\) from \(N\) with \(K\) special): \[P(X=k)=\frac{\binom Kk\binom{N-K}{n-k}}{\binom Nn},\quad\mu=nK/N\]

Q8 — Poisson Distribution [6 pts · Ch. 4–5]

A call center receives an average of 4 calls per hour.
  • (a) Find the probability of at most 2 calls in one hour.
  • (b) Find the probability of at least 1 call in one hour.
  • (c) Find the probability of exactly 1 call in a 15-minute window.

Q8 — Solutions

\(N\sim\text{Poisson}(4)\) for 1 hr;  \(N'\sim\text{Poisson}(1)\) for 15 min.
(a) \[P(N\le2)=e^{-4}(1+4+8)=13e^{-4}\]
\(\approx0.2381\)
(b) \[P(N\ge1)=1-e^{-4}\]
\(\approx0.9817\)
(c) \[P(N'=1)=e^{-1}\cdot1=e^{-1}\]
\(\approx0.3679\)

Q4 — Bulb Lifetime PDF [10 pts · Ch. 5]

The lifetime (days) of an incandescent bulb has PDF \[f(x)=\frac{5}{2}\,x^{-3/2},\quad x>25.\] A room has 8 independent bulbs. Find:
  • (a) \(P(\text{no bulb needs replacement in first 50 days})\)
  • (b) \(P(\text{exactly 4 bulbs need replacement in first 50 days})\)

Q4 — Solutions

Single-bulb survival: \[P(X>50)=\int_{50}^\infty\tfrac52\,x^{-3/2}\,dx=\frac{5}{\sqrt{50}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\approx0.7071\]
(a) All 8 survive: \[P=\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right)^{\!8}=\frac{1}{16}\]
\(=0.0625\)
(b) \(p=P(X\le50)=1-\frac1{\sqrt2}\approx0.2929\);  \(W\sim\text{Bin}(8,p)\). \[P(W=4)=\binom{8}{4}\!\left(1-\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\right)^{\!4}\!\left(\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\right)^{\!4} =\frac{70}{4}\!\left(1-\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\right)^{\!4}\]
\(\approx0.1288\)

Q6 — Normal Distribution [10 pts · Ch. 5]

Rod diameter: \(X\sim N(10,\,0.04)\) mm (\(\mu=10,\,\sigma=0.2\)). Defective if outside \([9.7,\,10.4]\). Find:
  • (a) \(P(9.8\le X\le10.3)\)
  • (b) \(P(\text{defective})\)
  • (c) 90th percentile of \(X\)
\(\Phi(1)\approx0.8413,\;\Phi(1.5)\approx0.9332,\;\Phi(2)\approx0.9772,\;z_{0.90}=1.282\)

Q6 — Solutions

(a) \(z_1=(9.8-10)/0.2=-1\), \(z_2=(10.3-10)/0.2=1.5\) \[P=\Phi(1.5)-\Phi(-1)=0.9332-0.1587\]
\(=0.7745\)
(b) \(z_3=-1.5\), \(z_4=2\) \[P(\text{defect})=\Phi(-1.5)+(1-\Phi(2))=0.0668+0.0228\]
\(=0.0896\approx8.96\%\)
(c) \(c=10+1.282\times0.2\)
\(\approx10.2564\text{ mm}\)

Ch. 6 Jointly Distributed Random Variables — Formulas

Marginals: \[f_X(x)=\int_{-\infty}^\infty f(x,y)\,dy, \qquad f_Y(y)=\int_{-\infty}^\infty f(x,y)\,dx\]
Independence: \[f(x,y)=f_X(x)\,f_Y(y)\text{ for all }x,y\]
Conditional PDF: \[f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=\frac{f(x,y)}{f_Y(y)}, \qquad E[X\mid Y=y]=\int x\,f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)\,dx\]
Covariance & Correlation: \[\text{Cov}(X,Y)=E[XY]-E[X]\,E[Y], \qquad \rho=\frac{\text{Cov}(X,Y)}{\sqrt{\text{Var}(X)\,\text{Var}(Y)}},\quad -1\le\rho\le1\]
Jacobian Transformation \((U,V)=g(X,Y)\): \[f_{U,V}(u,v)=f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,\left|\det\frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(u,v)}\right|\]

Q9 — Joint Exponential PDF [12 pts · Ch. 6]

Let \((X,Y)\) have joint PDF \(f(x,y)=C\,e^{-3x/2}e^{-2y}\), \(x,y>0\).
  • (a) Find \(C\).
  • (b) Find \(P(X\le2,\;Y>1)\).
  • (c) Find \(P(Y<2X)\).
  • (d) Find the conditional density \(f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)\).

Q9 — Solutions (a)–(b)

(a) PDF factors, so integrals separate: \[\iint Ce^{-3x/2}e^{-2y}\,dy\,dx=C\cdot\frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{2}=\frac{C}{3}=1\]
\(C=3\)
(b) \(f(x,y)=f_X(x)\cdot f_Y(y)\) with \(f_X=\tfrac32e^{-3x/2}\), \(f_Y=2e^{-2y}\); \(X\perp Y\). \[P(X\le2,\,Y>1)=(1-e^{-3})\cdot e^{-2}\]
\(\approx0.1286\)

Q9 — Solutions (c)–(d)

(c) \[P(Y<2X)=\int_0^\infty\!\int_0^{2x}3e^{-3x/2}e^{-2y}\,dy\,dx =\frac{3}{2}\!\left(\frac{2}{3}-\frac{2}{11}\right)=\frac{3}{2}\cdot\frac{16}{33}\]
\(=8/11\approx0.7273\)
(d) Marginal: \(f_Y(y)=2e^{-2y}\). \[f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=\frac{3e^{-3x/2}e^{-2y}}{2e^{-2y}}\]
\(=\tfrac{3}{2}e^{-3x/2},\quad x>0\)
(Independent of \(y\) — confirms \(X\perp Y\); \(X\sim\text{Exp}(3/2)\))
